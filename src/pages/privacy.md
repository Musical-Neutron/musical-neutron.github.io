_Last updated_: 12 October 2026

This is the personal website of Oliver Newton, an astrophysicist at the University of Surrey. This page explains what information is collected when you visit it and who receives it.

If you have questions about this notice, or want to ask about, correct or delete information you have sent through the site, email [o.newton@surrey.ac.uk](mailto:o.newton@surrey.ac.uk).

## Visiting the site

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes ([GitHub Pages: data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)).
- **Analytics.** The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and sends Google information such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can block it in your browser's privacy settings or with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it is remembered next time. It is not sent anywhere.

## Contact form

When you use the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you enter are sent to [Formspree](https://formspree.io), which stores them and forwards them by email. They are used only to reply to your enquiry. You can email instead if you prefer.

The form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which collects information about your browser and how you use the page to check that you are not a bot. Its use is subject to Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

## Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to other websites, such as journal articles, news coverage and the CWEBDWARFS project site. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
