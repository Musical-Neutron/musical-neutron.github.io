## Joining CFT in Warsaw

In September 2022 I moved to the [Center for Theoretical Physics (CFT)](https://www.cft.edu.pl/) of the Polish Academy of Sciences in Warsaw, to work with [Wojciech Hellwing](https://www.cft.edu.pl/pracownik/whellwing) and collaborators under the auspices of the COLAB project.

The focus at CFT is the **cosmic web** and its impact on the evolution of dark matter structure. In particular:

- How filaments, walls, and voids influence the formation and evolution of low‑mass haloes.
- Connecting the cosmic web in simulations with and without full physics.

This will help me expand my research interests attempting to understand the connection between galaxies and their environment.

### Life in Warsaw

As for my move to Lyon a few years ago, I'm looking forward to experiencing a new part of Europe I haven't had the opportunity to visit much before. Knowing Wojciech, I expect his group will be a vibrant and dynamic place to conduct research, and that there will be a lively exchange of ideas throughout my time at CFT!
