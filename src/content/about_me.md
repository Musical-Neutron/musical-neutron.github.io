I pursued my BSc MPhys in Physics at the [University of Warwick](https://warwick.ac.uk) followed by a year working in industry. My move to [Durham University](https://www.dur.ac.uk/) allowed me to complete my PhD at the [Institute for Computational Cosmology](http://www.icc.dur.ac.uk/) under the supervision of [Prof. Adrian Jenkins](https://www.durham.ac.uk/staff/a-r-jenkins/) and [Prof. Sir Carlos Frenk](https://www.durham.ac.uk/staff/c-s-frenk/).

My first postdoctoral position began in October 2019 in Lyon, France working with [Dr. Noam Libeskind](https://www.aip.de/en/members/noam-libeskind/) on the HESTIA suite of Local Group simulations. After this I undertook a short-term position at the [Astrophysics Research Institute](https://www.ljmu.ac.uk/research/centres-and-institutes/astrophysics-research-institute) in Liverpool, UK working with [Prof. Rob Crain](https://profiles.ljmu.ac.uk/8983-robert-crain) studying how the merger histories of _L_<sup>*</sup> galaxies affect their Globular Cluster populations. In September 2022 I moved to the [Center for Theoretical Physics](https://www.cft.edu.pl/) in Warsaw, Poland to work with [Dr. Wojciech Hellwing](https://www.cft.edu.pl/pracownik/whellwing) looking at the effects of cosmic environment on structure formation.

## Out of the office

I've played music with a variety of bands and orchestras from an early age, affording me opportunities to perform in venues across the UK and Europe, including multiple appearances in London's Royal Albert Hall. I was also fortunate to establish and support the [UniBrass Foundation](http://www.unibrass.co.uk/the-foundation/) as a trustee during its first six years. I occasionally try my hand at running and baking (not at the same time!).
